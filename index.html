About

Vision systems that inspect with less labels

I design scalable vision systems that combine synthetic anomaly generation, pseudo-anomaly insertion, and efficient deep learning architectures for robust, label-efficient visual inspection. My research contributions include PAI-Lib, TASAD, and multi-class anomaly detection frameworks validated on MVTec, VisA, and KolektorSDD2.
